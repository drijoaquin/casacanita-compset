Methodology: Weekly figures are PUBLISHED nightly asking rates blended from Booking.com and Expedia (2 adults, 1 night, the representative Saturday, pre-tax) for all eight hotels including Casa Canita — an apples-to-apples comparison of published rates. A hotel's weekly value is the average of the channels that returned a rate; weeks with no rate on either channel are shown as gaps (never zero). The comp-set average each week is the mean across the seven competitors that returned a rate and excludes Casa Canita. Casa Canita's realized run-rate ADR ($249, net of taxes & fees, pooled from the most recent three mature months in the pick-up report) is shown separately as a reference KPI and is NOT the weekly published line. Occupancy is intentionally not shown.
