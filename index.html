Methodology. Casa Canita ADR & occupancy are real PMS figures parsed from the pick-up report monthly summary (occupancy = room-nights ÷ 24 rooms × days; ADR = revenue ÷ room-nights), mapped onto forward weeks by month — these reflect bookings on the books (pace), not final occupancy. Comp-set ADR is a Booking.com + Expedia blend sampled on 3 Saturdays and linearly interpolated between (dashed = modeled). Comp-set occupancy is estimated from live availability probes (a market signal, not reported occupancy): sold-out dates = 99%, otherwise occ = 1 − min(10, rooms)/rooms. Run-rate reference = average of the 3 most recent mature months (Mar–May 2026).
